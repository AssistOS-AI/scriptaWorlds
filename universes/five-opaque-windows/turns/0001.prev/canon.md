# Canon — Five Opaque Windows

## Fundamental laws
- Prohibition that holds in this world: no one was allowed to solve the problem before they could describe who would pay for the solution
- The world is built from the following speculative operations of the Periodic Table of Ideas:
- Finitely Extensible: a universe where the system remains finite yet extensible (science fantasy and meta-reality, reflexivity).
- Substrate-Independent Person: a universe where identity is a pattern rather than privileged material (mind and identity, outfinite).

These laws are inviolable. Every important event in the book must be a consequence of them, and exceptions
need a cause already established in canon.
