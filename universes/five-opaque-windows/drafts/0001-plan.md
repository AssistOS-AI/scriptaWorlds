# Plan chapter 0001
- dramatic_question: Who will be written on the account as the one who pays for the first quiet room?
- anchor_character: Aster, eleven, who feels every unreadable region as a physical absence.
- character_want: She wants the quarrel with Vela repaired before Vela leaves the workshop.
- primary_idea: Unreadable region (mind-identity) — a piece of a patterned person can be made opaque to everybody, including its owner.
- human_need: To be let in by the people she loves, and to be allowed not to be read by them.
- opening_hook: The workshop's five windows are opaque and Aster likes the cold band where the glass went blind; Vela arrives with a licence form the Ledger has already sent back once, because the account names no payer who consents.
- beats:
  1. Aster sits on the sill of the third window feeling the winter's absence behind the blind glass; the workshop's board says the quiet room test is scheduled for the afternoon, and Vela brings back the licence form with the Ledger's refusal written on it.
  2. Vela's first account named Odile, the maker, as the payer: the maker loses the one student she cannot read. The Ledger rejects it, because the price must be felt by whoever is written on the line, and Aster can already feel where the real price will land.
  3. The quarrel underneath: at breakfast Aster read the transfer list out loud from the open ledger and Vela's name was on it before Vela had told anyone; on the sill they argue about whether thinking in the dark is a right or a lie, and about which shape of hiding to test.
  4. Odile sets the room — not built but subtracted, because the workshop has thirty-one rooms and always thirty-one — and signs the licence as first payer, then Vela signs as second; the hour of Vela's reasoning about the quarrel goes dark.
  5. Vela comes out with a conclusion and no reasons: she apologises in exact words she cannot defend, and Aster standing beside her feels the hour as a cold place where something of her friend should be.
  6. Aster writes her own name on the additional-payers line of the licence, and the Ledger returns it flagged: a price that cannot be described in advance cannot be consented to, and a review is scheduled; nothing is undone, because undoing it would need Vela's consent.
- decision: Whether the first quiet room hides a record (a locked drawer Vela can describe but cannot open) or a process (an hour of reasoning she keeps the conclusion of and can never audit), and who is written on the account as the payer of each.
- local_consequence: The workshop has its first legally hidden hour; Odile's own audit shows a blank with a duration in it; Aster learns that she is a payer who cannot be asked for consent in advance.
- long_horizon: In a hundred years every person in the sector carries a few unreadable hours, and the only surviving measure of a mind is what other people can feel missing in it.
- payoff: No earlier thread exists yet; the episode closes its own: the children's technical right is licensed and used once, honestly accounted for, without anyone being un-hidden.
- return_hook: The Ledger flags the third payer and schedules a review of the account.
- new_entities: Odile, the maker of the workshop (one new important character).
- deferred_answers: What Odile sees where the hidden hour should be — a blank or a shape — due_chapter: 3
