# Plan chapter 0001
- dramatic_question: Can Kesh close the Survey's largest node without writing Aster's hunger into the payer column?
- anchor_character: Kesh, surveyor of the relief room in the Earth-Library sector.
- character_want: To close the arrival node before the audit and keep his seat at the projection table.
- primary_idea: The relief map of recurring questions — the Survey's node for "the world next door" (knowledge-truth / compressive taxonomy).
- human_need: Safety — a post kept, a friend fed, a shelf not taken away.
- opening_hook: Kesh projects four hundred kilometres of the sector as terrain at the start of the shift; eleven nodes are due and every payer column is blank.
- beats:
  - Kesh lays the arrival node over the terrain and sees it would compress sixty-one thousand titles into one question, clearing his quarter at a stroke.
  - The Survey returns the whitening forecast: twelve shelves become white for anyone with White Hunger in the sector; Kesh knows one such reader.
  - Aster asks him, over an orange in the reading room, to leave the small bench — two hundred and seventeen unsorted books — out of whatever he closes.
  - At the ledger desk, Kesh learns that the sector's payer column has stood in Aster's house since the Verne Removal; her hunger is what keeps the shelves lit.
  - Kesh signs the node in her house's name and files the small bench as unresolved, at a permanent cost to the Survey's own rating.
  - The node closes at the fourth hour; twelve shelves whiten, the small bench stays readable, and the projection grows an unnamed peak where the exception was written.
- decision: Sign the node with Aster's house in the payer column and carve out the small bench, or leave the node open and let the sector's payer column lapse into a Removal.
- local_consequence: Twelve shelves whiten for Aster while the two hundred and seventeen books of the small bench stay readable; the Survey records its first exception.
- long_horizon: +10 years — the exceptions are the only unclassified text left in the sector, and White Hunger becomes a paid work rather than a private appetite.
- payoff: Aster's request to keep the small bench unsorted is answered in the same episode: the bench is filed unresolved and stays black on the page.
- return_hook: The projection grows an unnamed peak over the exception, as if the Survey had begun to ask about the thing it was told to leave alone.
- new_entities: Kesh (surveyor, anchor), Aster. Minor: Dorel, the ledger clerk.
- deferred_answers: def-0001 — who signed the sector's payer column before Aster's house, and why the first line is unreadable — asked now, due_chapter 3, because the name is the load-bearing fact of the book.
