# Plan chapter 0006

- dramatic_question: Who on Hesper has the right to choose the dead child whose name the flowering will carry, and who has to say that name out loud at the roots?
- anchor_character: Officer Rusk, with Verrin, the oldest hand of the feed crew, carrying the price.
- character_want: Rusk wants the flowering answered before the ring loses its nerve, and he wants the voice at the roots to be his, as his own line of chapter 3 promised.
- primary_idea: A name is a debt of memory and not of paper: the farm can give the flower only a name that a living hand still holds, so the choosing becomes a search for the last rememberer rather than a page out of the register (Atlas: unspeakable-name, paper-law).
- human_need: Meaning — the need for the dead to have been worth something, and for a farm's silence to be a thing it knows and not a hole in it.
- opening_hook: Rusk calls the whole farm to the root wall before the first shift, the first choosing Hesper has ever held, with the sign standing, the count carrying nothing and the book carrying no line for a choosing.
- beats:
  - Rusk puts the problem to the ring: the law says the flowering is given a name, the book has no line for a choosing, and Meren says in front of the crew that no child has died on the farm in nine years; the boy from the water crew proposes the cheap answer, the oldest name in the register that no hand remembers.
  - Meren sends Bea down to the stores without the book and without her, and Iven draws the two signs he read: eighteen years ago one hook at the end, nine years ago none; Bea reads the end of the stroke as the tip of the blood sign turned back on itself three times, says the plant counts nothing and the three days are the farm's, which takes the clock off the choosing.
  - Iven answers the child's question: nine years ago he wrote under his reading a line promising that the farm would keep the child, the farm then took her off the page, the slate and the walls, the line failed in three days and the book took his tooth; a line is a debt whether it is a request or a promise.
  - The crew makes its first rule of choosing: a name may be given only by a hand that remembers the child, said out loud at the roots, once, never by the officer and never by the book; Rusk gives up the voice his own line promised.
  - The register is carried up and the ring counts six stubs where six flowering years were cut out of the book of the farm's dead; nobody can put a face to a surviving name except Verrin, who was seven in the same bunk row, and she sets her three terms.
  - Verrin says the name once over the bare soil where the four hands went in, the plant does not move for it by so much as a leaf, Rusk writes and signs a line that asks for nothing, and Iven takes the year's page down the spine to cut it out.
- decision: Whether the farm spends an unremembered name out of the register, which costs nobody anything and teaches the ring that names are paper, or binds itself to spend only a name a living hand still holds, which wounds one old woman and will be impossible at the next flowering. The crew takes the second and Verrin names her own terms for it; the reader decides what the farm does with a silence it has chosen to know.
- local_consequence: Hesper holds its first choosing; the voice of the naming leaves the officer's mouth and goes to the rememberer; the register's six holes are counted aloud and the spending is written into the green journal; the plant does not move for the name, and the flower is found to be looking past the blood at the seat where the root line enters the soil.
- long_horizon: +9 years — the only hand that holds the new name is dead, and the farm must break the rule it made this morning or refuse the flowering; +100 years — a farm that keeps a record of what it spent instead of a record of what it lost.
- payoff: thread-0006 (what the three hooks ask), def-0002 (the line Iven wrote nine years ago), prom-0002 (Iven drawing the signs of the flowerings he read), and the naming the law has been owed since the flower opened.
- return_hook: The open flower is not aimed at the dark patch where the blood went in; it is a hand's width past it, at the seat where the root line comes up out of the manifold into the soil.
- new_entities: Verrin, the oldest hand of the feed crew, 45 years in the trench (the only new important character); the child she remembers is never named in the text.
- deferred_answers: Whether the name said once at the roots can be kept out of the farm's speech for the rest of Verrin's life, and what the law does if it is said again — due_chapter 8.
