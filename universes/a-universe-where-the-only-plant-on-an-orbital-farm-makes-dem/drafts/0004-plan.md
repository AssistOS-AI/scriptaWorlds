# Plan chapter 0004
- dramatic_question: Can Bea learn to read a whole shape before the count takes Rusk's eye, and what is the old Reader refusing to explain about the last flowering?
- anchor_character: Bea, the Reader of Hesper, seven years old.
- character_want: To be able to read the sign standing over the shelf, and to know whose name the farm is being asked to say.
- primary_idea: A reader trained on whole shapes instead of chart words (Atlas node: child-readers / reading by experiment), and the debt of a line that falls on the name under it.
- human_need: Meaning — a child who must carry a decision adults are afraid of, and an old man who wants the child not to become him.
- opening_hook: On the morning the officer's finger is missing, the ring goes back to work and Meren takes Bea and the folded chart down the spine to the stores, because three days from that morning there is an eye.
- beats:
  - Meren brings Bea and the old chart to Iven's stores and asks him to teach the child to read shapes; he refuses, because the chart was how he stopped the farm from reading leaves.
  - He agrees to one lesson and folds every word under its sign; Bea reads a sign whose ending is hidden and says "I do not know", and he tells her that a shape can tell you no.
  - Bea reads the name sign with three hooks as one line bent three times and says she does not think it counts; Iven goes quiet and says he drew no sign he was not sure of.
  - Meren asks whose name was given nine years ago; Iven admits the line that year was his own writing, refuses to say how the name was chosen, and the woman from the second shift says the name the plant carries is her daughter's.
  - On the second morning, at the wall, Meren drills the child in refusing a reading while the crew waits for an answer; the farm writes nothing until the whole sign has been read in the light.
  - In the evening Bea goes down the spine alone with her slate and Iven draws the sign of the last flowering: the flower sign, the long stroke, and no hooks.
- decision: none — this episode honours a request to watch a lesson; the decision for the reader is in the offer.
- local_consequence: The farm stops answering a sign it has not read whole; the child's own reading of chapter 3 is named as half a reading; the crew learns whose daughter's name the plant carries, and Iven's tooth is shown to have been paid on a line he wrote himself.
- long_horizon: +10 years — a Reader trained on shapes instead of charts, and an officer who signs every line he writes; +100 years — a farm that can tell an unanswered question from an order.
- payoff: thread-0002 (what name the crew will give) and deferred answer def-0001 (which dead child gave the plant its name) are both answered.
- return_hook: Iven draws the sign of the last flowering and it had no hooks.
- new_entities: none; the woman from the second shift is an existing character who becomes important.
- deferred_answers: What line did Iven write in the green journal nine years ago, and what did he read at that flowering — due_chapter 6.
