# Plan chapter 0001

- dramatic_question: Will the plant stop shaking before the third day ends, and who has to bleed to make it stop?
- anchor_character: Doctor Meren, the physician of the orbital farm Hesper.
- character_want: To stop any cut today and to open the rusted root feed line before one of the three unbandaged arms is lost.
- primary_idea: A leaf sign carries more than one meaning — the sign for blood and the sign for iron in the water are the same stroke with one ending — and children read the whole shape while adults read the letters they were taught.
- human_need: Safety: the need not to pay with the body for another person's certainty.
- opening_hook: Three arms lie unbandaged in the infirmary on the third morning of a blood request, and the plant has still not stopped shaking.
- beats:
  1. Meren refuses the third cut; Officer Rusk brings the green journal and offers his own scarred arm.
  2. Rusk asks the seven-year-old Reader, Bea, to choose: keep the journal's wording or say a new reading aloud in front of the crew.
  3. Bea re-reads the sign from the leaves instead of the old Reader's chart and finds the dash: iron in the water, not blood.
  4. Meren bets her own hand on it and cracks the root feed line open: brown water, a valve seat eaten through, two refused requisitions.
  5. The clean bypass is opened, the plant stops shaking before dusk, and the three arms are bandaged; no tooth is taken.
  6. Rusk writes the new rule in the green journal; at dusk Bea copies a new sign that Rusk finds in the back of the book, beside the word "flower".
- decision: Whether to trust a child's re-reading of a sign against the written journal before the three-day deadline runs out.
- local_consequence: The plant stops shaking without blood, nobody loses a tooth, the old adult chart comes down from the wall, and from now on the officer writes the reader's own words into the green journal.
- long_horizon: In ten years the farm reads every request from the leaves through a child's voice, and the old practice of cutting on an adult's wording is remembered as a disease the crew used to have.
- payoff: The three-day blood request is closed inside this episode: the plant was never asking for blood.
- return_hook: At dusk the leaves show the flowering sign for the first time in nine years, and Bea asks whose name the crew will give this time.
- new_entities: Doctor Meren and the child Reader Bea (the two important new characters); Officer Rusk as the opposing authority; the farm Hesper (place). The plant has no spoken name.
- deferred_answers: The name the plant carries from the last flowering, and whose dead child it was — due_chapter 4.
