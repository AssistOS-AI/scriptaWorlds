# Plan chapter 0010
- dramatic_question: Will the old Reader finally draw every sign of the last flowering for the child — including the one shape he left off the chart — and say out loud what his tooth was charged for?
- anchor_character: Bea, seven, the Reader of Hesper, who goes down the spine alone on the first quiet morning with nothing under her arm.
- character_want: To hold the whole of the last flowering before the only hand that read it is dead, and to know who pays for the words she speaks at the shelf.
- primary_idea: A drawing is not a line: a sheet that carries every shape of a flowering with no word under any of them can hold a farm's memory and charge nobody, which is the first page on Hesper that costs nothing (Atlas node `chart-without-words`).
- human_need: Meaning — to belong to a place that can keep its own dead in something other than a book that charges a hand.
- opening_hook: The twenty-third morning has no sign in it either; the doctor reads the wheel at the seat and writes the same number as the day before, and the child says she is going down the spine alone to collect the signs the old Reader promised her.
- beats:
  - At the stores the child asks for every sign of the last flowering and the old Reader refuses, because a chart is a shape with a word under it and his chart is what put iron back into blood; she answers with the law itself — the law charges lines, and nothing has ever been charged for a drawing — and adds that the wall is flat, so nothing she reads this morning can cost the farm anything.
  - He sets his price: he draws and she looks, no word on the paper and no word out of her mouth, because a word under a shape makes a chart again; she takes it and keeps the one sentence he allows her, *I do not know*.
  - He draws the three signs of that year on the bare back of the old chart: the long stroke with the dash of water, the two bowls of the crown lamps, and the flower with the long stroke under it and nothing at its end — the shape he gave a word to, and under that word a promise that the farm would keep the child it was naming.
  - What he did not say at the wall on the fifteenth day: the officer had given him the taking the same morning — the register page, the slate and every wall — so the promise went into the one page the taking did not cover, and it was already broken when he wrote it; the book does not read intentions, and on the third morning it took his tooth, which came out whole and which he has kept folded under the chart ever since.
  - What he finally says it was for: nine years ago the pen was his hand and the book charged his hand; since the spring the farm writes the child's words through the officer and puts the officer's name under them, so the tooth, the finger and the eye were not the plant's doing but the price of the rule the farm made to keep the child safe — and there is still nothing on the farm's ladder above an eye.
  - The shape he never drew: in the weeks after that flower closed, the small turned shape came up in the leaves along the pipe at the seat, and he had no word for it and drew nothing he had no word for; it went the year after and he does not know what ended it, and the child tells him it came back on the twenty-first day on the same pipe.
  - At the wall the child lays the wordless sheet on the shelf beside the book and gives the ring the two facts she has; the ring keeps the pen and takes the names out of the green journal, states out loud that its silence around the name it gave is its own and not a price, and answers the woman from the second shift.
- decision: Whether the farm keeps a pen that charges the officer for the child's voice so that no reading is ever written in a child's hand, or takes its names out of the book altogether; the ring takes the names out, keeps the pen, and leaves the seventh name standing, because the only way to take a name out of the book is one more line with a hand under it.
- local_consequence: Hesper has a page of its own signs with no words on it, kept on the shelf beside the green journal; the book will carry the farm's days, its water and its numbers and no name of a dead child again; the farm has said out loud that the silence about the child it gave the plant is its own choice; and the seventh name stands in the emptied book with the price of removing it now stated in front of the ring.
- long_horizon: At +9 years the next flowering asks for a name and the farm has a sheet of shapes it drew itself and a book with no names in it; at +100 years the farm's record of what it spent on the plant is a wordless sheet and a register of holes, and the only readable page left is the one nobody wrote a word on.
- payoff: thread-0012, the pen and the names in the book, answered with a rule the green journal does not carry; thread-0015, the mother's request, answered at the wall; and the long mystery of the Reader's tooth of nine years ago, whose cause is now said out loud by the hand that paid it.
- return_hook: The officer puts the seventh name back in front of the ring — it stands in the book the farm has just emptied of names, the farm may not say it, and taking it out costs one more line and one more hand.
- new_entities: none.
- deferred_answers: What the law does with a line that comes true after its count has run out, since the promise of nine years ago is the first line on Hesper that the keeping could still keep — due_chapter 13.
