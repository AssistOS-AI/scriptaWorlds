# Plan chapter 0002

- dramatic_question: Can Officer Rusk answer the flowering with an adult's wording in the green journal, and what does the plant do with it?
- anchor_character: Officer Rusk, sixty, the officer of Hesper, who writes the green journal.
- character_want: To write one line the farm can act on before the flower opens, and to keep the plant's air, water and power in his own hand instead of in a seven-year-old's chalk.
- primary_idea: A line written in the green journal binds the person who wrote it, not the plant — the plant answers only what is done to it.
- human_need: Authority: the need to be the one who answers for the cost, and to be seen answering for it.
- opening_hook: The buds of the flowering open overnight, the air gauge of the work ring loses a tenth, and under the flower sign the leaves hold a small shape the Reader refuses to read.
- beats:
  1. Day 4: the flowering begins and the ring loses air; the small shape appears and Bea says she does not know it and asks for a hand lamp; Rusk reads the shape from the chart he carries in his head as blood again.
  2. Rusk writes the adult line in the green journal, with his own name under it; Meren tells him the count will run out on him and he says then it will be his; in the low ring the old Reader tells him what a tooth is like.
  3. The farm does not cut: the boy from the water crew offers his hand, Meren holds Bea's promise in front of everybody, and the plant keeps repeating the shape while the flower stays half open and the air drops again.
  4. Bea reads the shape by experiment: she holds a hand lamp to the wall and every leaf of the crown turns to it, so the two hooks are the two bowl shades of the crown lamps, and the plant is asking for light.
  5. Rusk decides: the lamp arms come off the work ring and go up the spine to the crown, the farm's power margin is spent, the light comes up, the plant stops shaking and the flower opens.
  6. On the fourth morning Rusk wakes without a tooth although the plant got what it asked for, and the open flower turns to its next request, a name, which the farm has no dead child to give.
- decision: Whose name the crew can give the flower now that no child has died on Hesper in nine years.
- local_consequence: The officer loses a tooth for a line the plant never read, the work ring burns dark for a shift and the farm's power margin is gone, the crew is split over what the tooth proved, and the plant stops shaking and flowers without a single cut.
- long_horizon: In a century the officers of Hesper keep a book that nothing answers, and the post of the Reader is written into the charter as the only instrument of the law.
- payoff: Meren's promise to Bea holds under the officer's own pressure, and the farm keeps the rule that the officer writes the reader's own words.
- return_hook: The flower is open and will ask for a name, and the only name the farm has is the one it is forbidden to say.
- new_entities: Iven, the Reader before Bea, who works in the low ring and reads nothing now (one minor character). The crown lamps of the root wall.
- deferred_answers: none new in this episode; the naming of the flower keeps its existing due date at chapter 4.
