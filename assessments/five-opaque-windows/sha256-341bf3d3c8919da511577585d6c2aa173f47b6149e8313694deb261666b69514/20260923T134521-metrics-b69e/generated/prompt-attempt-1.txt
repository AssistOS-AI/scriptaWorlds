  1 — mostly failed: The requirement is met only intermittently; recovery is usually blocked, so the reader must supply what the text does not give.
  2 — partial: The requirement is met in places; recovery is possible but effortful, uneven or dependent on one fortunate cue.
  3 — reliable: The requirement is reliably met across the selection; the reader recovers the relation or the feature without strain, with at most minor gaps.
  4 — exact and economical: The requirement is met exactly and without waste; every needed relation or feature is recoverable and deliberate choices serve it rather than merely avoiding failure.
